Read Ahead

Press play, listen to the count-in, and keep going. Each bar is covered the moment you reach its downbeat, so you have to read the next one while you play from memory.

While paused, click a covered bar to peek at it; click again to cover it. Resuming counts you in and restarts the bar you paused in. Shortcuts: Space play/pause, R restart, N new exercise.
