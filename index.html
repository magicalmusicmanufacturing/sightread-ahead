Read Ahead

Press play, listen to the count-in, and keep going. Each bar is covered the moment you reach its downbeat, so you have to read the next one while you play from memory.

While paused, click a covered bar to peek at it; click again to cover it. Resuming counts you in at the bar you paused in. To start somewhere else, click any uncovered bar or use Start at bar. Shortcuts: Space play/pause, R restart, N new exercise. With a MIDI keyboard connected, your notes are checked as you play: at the end, green notes were right and red ones were missed or wrong.
